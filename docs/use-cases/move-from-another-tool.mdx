You are leaving another DAM, a brand portal tool or a shared Drive. Artbucket
has no connector that pulls from Frontify, Bynder, Brandfolder or Google
Drive. You download your files from the old tool and bring them in. This page
says what comes across and what you redo.

## Bring the files in

Pick one of these, by how much you have.

### A few hundred files

Drop the folder into **Assets**, or open **New** in the
sidebar and choose **Upload a folder**. Every file inside lands in the
library; the folders themselves don't become collections. Make the
collections you need afterwards and add files to them.

### Your brand kit, with its folders

If your server offers it, open
**Brands**, **New brand** and choose **Import your brand**. Upload a `.zip` of
everything, or just the guidelines PDF, and choose **Upload and read**.
Artbucket reads it into a plan before anything is added:

- the zip's folders become collections,
- a brand book in it is read into rules (colors, typography, logos, voice) and
  draft guideline pages,
- logos can be cut from the book when there is no separate file.

Review the plan, untick what you don't want, and choose **Apply the plan**.
Opening a plan takes Contributor, applying it Editor. Changed your mind? **Undo
import** removes what it added, within 30 days.

### Thousands of files

Ask a developer to script it with the command line:

```bash
npx artbucket login app.artbucket.io --scope write
npx artbucket ingest ./export/*.jpg --collection {collection-id} --tag spring-2026
```

`ingest` takes files or public URLs. The same file uploaded twice is stored
once.

## What comes across

- Titles, captions, credits, copyright and keywords written inside the files
  (EXIF, IPTC and XMP) are read on upload. Keywords become tags.
- Content Credentials (C2PA) are kept.
- Figma and Google Docs links can be added as links, with **Add a link** in
  the **New** menu.

## What you redo

- Custom metadata: fields your old tool kept outside the files, like a
  campaign or a region, don't travel. Define them in **Settings**, **Custom
  fields**, then fill them in. A collection can carry values its files inherit.
- Licenses: enter each licensed photo's terms in its **Rights** fold, so
  the use check works. See [Check a photo](/use-cases/check-a-photo).
- Guidelines: unless the import read your PDF, set the brand up as rules:
  [Set up your brand](/use-cases/set-up-your-brand) takes an afternoon. An AI
  tool connected as an **Editor** can read the PDF and write the rules for you to
  check.
- Links: links into the old tool stop working when you close it, and
  nothing redirects them. Replace them with Artbucket links, which end in
  `/c/` and keep working through new versions.
- People: invite them in **Settings**, **Members**.

## When it worked

Search in **Assets** for a keyword from your old tool and the files come up with
their tags. The Brand Agent Score on the brand's **Overview** shows what the
brand still lacks.
