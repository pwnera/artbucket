The logo was redrawn. You want the new one on the portal, in the decks, on
the website and in every AI tool, and you don't want to email forty people a
zip. You upload it once, as a new version of the old logo, and chase only
what kept a copy.

## Steps

1. Find the current logo: search for it in **Assets** and open it.
2. In its **Versions** fold, choose **New version**, then **Upload, and make it
   current**. Pick **Upload as a draft** instead if the new logo shouldn't go
   out yet, say for a launch date. A Contributor sees **Suggest a new
   version**: their file waits in **Review** until an Editor approves it.
3. Check the **Used in** fold on the asset. It lists the brand rules that
   point at the old file, the pages and portals that show it, and the sites
   that load it.
4. Open each brand rule listed there (the brand's **Rules** tab, then the
   rule's details) and choose the new version under **Assets**. Then choose
   **Release** in the brand's header, so the guideline pages, portals and
   BrandHub show the new logo.

## What follows on its own

As soon as the new version is current:

- Every `/c/` link to the logo leads to the new file within a minute: the
  embeds on websites, in docs and emails, and the links copied from **Sizes
  and formats**.
- Share links and the collections on portals show the new version and drop
  the old one.
- Your own PowerPoint, Word and Excel templates on **Brand files** swap the old
  logo image for the new one in each download. The templates Artbucket makes
  from the brand follow the logo rule, which step 4 changes.
- **Can I use this?** and agents' use checks refuse the old file and name the
  new one instead.

## What doesn't

<Note>
On Artbucket Cloud, **Fixes** and **Notify**, and the templates on
**Brand files**, are on the Team and Business plans. A server of your own has
all of it.
</Note>

Copies made before the change: a PNG pasted into last year's deck, a file an
agency downloaded, a site that stores its own copy. Artbucket can't reach
those files, but it sees who keeps loading the old version from it.

1. Open **Inbox** in the sidebar, then **Fixes**. Each site or agent still loading the old
   logo is a row.
2. Choose **Notify** on a row. Artbucket emails whoever answers for that site
   or agent, with a `/c/` link that always leads to the current version, so
   the next change reaches them without another email. For a site elsewhere,
   type the address of the person to tell.
3. The row then says when it was notified. Each version and place is told
   once.

**Insights** shows the same under **Where from**, **Old versions**, and the
brand's **Insights** shows how many fetches are on the latest release.

## If you got it wrong

Open the asset's **Versions** fold and choose **Make current** on the old
version. Every `/c/` link goes back within a minute.
