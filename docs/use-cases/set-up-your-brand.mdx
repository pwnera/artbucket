Today your brand is a logo folder and a PDF that nobody opens. This page gets
it into Artbucket as rules, with guideline pages, released to a portal your
team can open.

## Before you start

Gather these, so you aren't hunting for them halfway through:

- The logo as SVG or PNG, and the version for dark backgrounds.
- Your colors as hex values.
- The names of your fonts, or the font files if they aren't on Google Fonts.
- A sentence or two on how the brand sounds, and words to avoid.
- Your current guidelines PDF, if there is one.

## Steps

1. Put the files in the library. Drop the logos and font files anywhere in
   Explore. For a Google font, open **New** in the sidebar and choose
   **Import a Google font**.
2. Open **Brands** in the sidebar and choose **New brand**.
3. Pick a start under **Start here**. If your website publishes a brand.json,
   choose **From a domain**, type the domain and choose **Look it up**.
   Otherwise choose **Blank**, or one of the templates if you want pages to
   edit rather than an empty brand. Then choose **Create brand**.
   If your server offers it, **Import your brand** is a fourth choice: it reads
   a zip of your brand files or your guidelines PDF into a plan you review
   first. See [Move from another tool](/use-cases/move-from-another-tool).
4. Open the brand's **Rules** tab. Under **Still missing**, choose **The main
   color**, **A heading face**, **The logo** and **The voice** one at a time,
   and fill each in. **Add a rule** adds the rest: more colors, a text face,
   words to avoid.
5. Open the logo rule's details and choose **Pick from the library** to attach
   the logo file. For the dark-background logo, add a version under
   **Versions** with the context `dark-background` and attach that file to it.
   Do the same for a color that changes on dark backgrounds.
6. Lay out the pages. Choose **Edit** in the brand's header, then **With an
   agent**: copy the MCP address and the example prompt into Claude or
   another assistant, and it drafts the pages from your rules. A template or
   an import already comes with pages.
7. Pick a look in **Theme**, in the brand's menu.
8. Choose **Release** in the brand's header. Write a note for readers and read
   **What readers get**. Under **Who should see it**, pick **People in this
   project** to give the brand a portal of its own. Choose **Release**.

## What you see

The confirmation says **Released** and lists the portal under **It shows on**.
Open it: your team reads the pages there, signed in. Nothing you edit from now
on reaches them until the next release.

The brand's **Overview** shows the Brand Agent Score, out of 100, and the
steps it still lacks: Colors, Typefaces, Logo, Voice, Look, Pages, Released
and Shared. When they are all done, the brand is set up.
