You started on Free and someone asks whether you need to pay. The answer
depends on four things: how much you store, how many people edit, how many
brands you keep, and whether you need your own branding or single sign-on.
Prices, monthly and yearly, are on
[artbucket.io/pricing](https://artbucket.io/pricing).

## What each plan holds

| | Free | Team | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Storage | 2 GB | 100 GB | 1 TB |
| Editors | 2 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Brands | 1 | 5 | 25 |
| Projects | 1 | 3 | Unlimited |
| Custom domains | 1 | 5 | 20 |
| Emails a day | 20 | 300 | 1,000 |
| Site build minutes from GitHub, a month | None | 500 | 2,500 |

Team and Business have every feature. Free leaves these out:

- Your own name and look on the app, portals and emails.
- Single sign-on.
- Rebuilding a site when the brand is released.
- **Automations**: emails and Slack or Teams messages when something happens.
- **Fixes**, with **Notify** and **Request it**: the list of places on old
  versions, searches that found nothing and licenses ending.
- **Templates**: share cards and PowerPoint, Word and Excel templates. A public
  BrandHub listing keeps its own share card and press kit on Free.
- The weekly digest email.

A server of your own has all of them, with no plan.

Team can add editors one at a time, up to 50 more, and Team and Business can
add storage in packs of 50 GB. For more than 25 brands, write to
hello@artbucket.io.

## Who counts as an editor

Only people with the Editor or Admin role, anywhere in the organization.
Viewers, Contributors, portal visitors and people who open a share link are
never counted. An agency that only downloads files costs nothing.

## Pick one

- One brand, two people looking after it, a kit of logos and fonts: Free.
- A company with sub-brands, a handful of editors, or a portal on its own
  domain per brand: Team.
- An agency running brands for clients, or a large photo library: Business.

## Change your plan

You need to be an admin of the organization.

1. Open **Settings**, **Usage** to see what you use against each limit.
2. Choose **Manage plan**. On Free, **Upgrade** in the header goes to the same
   place.
3. Pick the plan, monthly or yearly, and any add-ons, then pay through Stripe.

You land back on **Usage**, which says the plan is active. The plans page also
changes add-ons, switches plans and opens the card, invoices and cancelling.

## At a limit

The app refuses the one thing that would go past it, such as an upload or an
invitation, and says which limit and where to manage the plan. Nothing already
there is taken away.

## When a plan ends

The organization goes back to Free's limits. If it holds less than 2 GB,
that is all.

If it holds more:

1. For 30 days everything works. The app shows a banner with the dates, and
   the buyer and the admins get reminders by email.
2. After 30 days the organization is read-only. Portals stay up.
3. After 60 days, the newest files past 2 GB are deleted, the oldest kept. They
   can be restored for 30 days after that.

Taking a plan again, or getting under 2 GB, stops all of it at once.

Each person can be the admin of one organization on Free. A second one needs
a plan.
