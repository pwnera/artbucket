---
title: Give an agency or partner the logos
sidebarTitle: Give partners the logos
description: Pick the right way to hand files to people outside the team, and get their work back for review.
---

An agency needs the logo pack today, and a photographer will send you pictures
back next week. Each needs a different way in, and neither needs your whole
library.

## Pick the way

| You want to | Use | They get | It stops |
|---|---|---|---|
| Send a few files once | A share link | A page to look and download, no account | On its last day, or when you revoke it |
| Keep a press kit or partner hub up | A portal | Your guideline pages and collections, at their own address | When you close it, or on a date you set |
| Work with them inside Artbucket | An invitation to one collection | An account that sees that collection and nothing else | When you remove their access |
| Get files back from them | An upload link | A page to send files through, no account | On its last day, or when you revoke it |

Share links and portals only hand out approved files that are still licensed.
A file stops being served the day after its license's last day of use.

## Send a share link

1. Open the collection and choose the share icon beside its name, or
   **Share** on one asset.
2. Under **Last day**, pick when it stops. Add a **Password** if the files are
   sensitive, and the partner's address under **Send it to**.
3. Choose **Make link**.

They get an email with the link. You see it under **Its link** in the same
dialog, and every link of the project is in **Settings**, **People**, **Links**.

## Open a portal

1. Open **Sites** in the sidebar, then **New site** and **Brand portal**.
2. Give it a **Name**. Under **Brands whose pages it shows**, pick the brand,
   and under **Collections, in its Assets view**, the collections they may
   download from.
3. Under **Who gets in**, pick **Whoever has the password** for an agency, or
   **Anyone with the address** for a press kit.
4. Under **Open until**, set a date if the work has an end. Choose **Make it**.

People without the password can ask for access. When someone asks, the
portal's row on **Sites** shows how many are waiting, and **Inbox**, **Requests**
lists them beside every other site's. Approve them from either. Each approved person gets a link of their
own by email.

## Invite them to one collection

1. Open **Settings**, **People** and choose **Invite people**.
2. Enter their **Email**, pick the collection under **On**, and pick
   **Viewer**, or **Contributor** if they should upload, under **As**.
3. Choose **Send invitation**. The link works once, for a week.

## Get their work back

Open **New** in the sidebar and choose **Request uploads by link**. Send the
link to the photographer or the agency. What they upload waits in **Review**,
out of the library, until an Editor approves it.

## Take it back

Revoke a link from the share dialog or **Settings**, **People**, **Links**: it stops at
once. On **Sites**, a portal's menu has **Take offline**. Files already
downloaded stay downloaded, so give out only what the license allows.
