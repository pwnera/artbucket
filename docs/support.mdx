## Artbucket Cloud

Support for [Artbucket Cloud](/cloud) is by email.

- **Questions, billing and your account:** [hello@artbucket.io](mailto:hello@artbucket.io).
  Name your organization and, for a problem, the page or URL and what you did.
- **Abuse on a portal or a BrandHub listing** (phishing, malware, someone
  else's brand or files): [abuse@artbucket.io](mailto:abuse@artbucket.io),
  with the address. A listing's owner can also be reached from **Report** on
  its BrandHub page ([Report, claim and delist](/guides/portals#report-claim-and-delist)).
- **Security:** as for the open source, below.

## Open source

- **Questions and bugs:** [GitHub issues](https://github.com/pwnera/artbucket/issues).
  Include the version you run (the image tag, or the release you checked out),
  what you did, and what you expected.
- **Security:** never in a public issue. Report through
  [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/pwnera/artbucket/security/advisories/new);
  see [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/pwnera/artbucket/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- **What is coming:** the [roadmap](https://github.com/pwnera/artbucket/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)
  is opinionated about order, and some things are deferred on purpose with a
  stated trigger.
- **Contributing:** read [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/pwnera/artbucket/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  first, and open an issue before a large change.

Pwnera SAS runs the project: a clear report with steps to reproduce gets fixed
fastest.
