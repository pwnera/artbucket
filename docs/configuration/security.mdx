## Rate limits

`/api` allows `RATE_LIMIT` requests a minute (1200 by default) per client
address, API keys included. Past it, a `429` with
`Retry-After`. Signing in has its own, stricter limits. A share link's password
allows ten wrong guesses in ten minutes, then waits, whoever is guessing. A
portal's password allows ten from one address and two hundred in all in ten
minutes; only wrong ones count.

The counts live in each process's memory. Behind several instances each counts
on its own; for a hard limit, put one in your reverse proxy too. Asset URLs
(`/a/`) are not limited: put a CDN in front of them on a public install, which
also caches renditions. What a rendition URL can cost is bounded instead: sizes
and quality snap to a few steps, a few renditions are made at once per server
with a short line behind them (past it, a `429`), and one that takes longer
than 30 seconds stops (a `413`). Uploads being filed take turns too, by size,
since each is read whole.

A rendition your team asks for counts toward its organization's storage. One
asked by someone a public asset or a signed URL alone let in is kept without
counting, since the organization doesn't choose what they ask, and bounded
instead:

- What pages draw, a width in WebP (`w_800,f_webp`), and the presets (the
  app's and portals' downloads) are always made.
- Anything else, up to 16 per file while the bucket keeps them; past that,
  a `403` until some expire. Renditions already made are always served, and
  a member who opens the URL signed in makes what they like.
- AVIF and drawn SVG, whose encoding and drawing don't stop at the time
  limit, at most 4096 px a side.

Deleting an asset stops its renditions counting at once.

The server takes the client's address from `X-Forwarded-For`. Run it behind a
reverse proxy that sets that header, and don't expose its port directly.

## Headers

Every page carries a Content-Security-Policy (only scripts carrying that
response's nonce run, frames from Figma and Google's embeds only, uploads to
your storage only),
`X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, a strict referrer
policy, a Permissions-Policy that turns off camera, microphone and location,
and `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin`. With an `https` `APP_URL`, HSTS
too.

## Uploaded files

Anyone with upload rights can upload an SVG or an HTML file, and `/a/{id}`
serves it from the app's own origin. It is served with a sandboxing policy, so
opened directly it runs no script and reaches nothing. PDFs are the exception,
since browsers show them in a viewer the sandbox would stop.

Writes signed by a session cookie are taken only from `APP_URL`'s origin.
Fetches from a URL (`ingest_asset`, `POST /api/v1/assets` with `url`) refuse
loopback, private and link-local addresses, checked on every redirect.

## Telemetry

None. Artbucket sends nothing anywhere. The Docker image sets
`NEXT_TELEMETRY_DISABLED=1`, and better-auth's telemetry is off. For a build
from source, set `NEXT_TELEMETRY_DISABLED=1` too (the systemd unit in
[Bare VPS](/installation/vps) does).
