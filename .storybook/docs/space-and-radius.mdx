# Space and radius

## Space

Tailwind's spacing, every step a multiple of <Token name="--spacing" />. Controls sit 2 apart (`gap-2`), fields and
groups 4 to 6, sections 8 and more. Widths below are what the browser computed.

<Spacing />

## Radius

One radius, <Token name="--radius" />, and steps around it (`globals.css`): `sm` and `md` for small controls and
fields, `lg` for cards, buttons and menus, `xl` for dialogs and tiles, `full` for pills and avatars. A brand site's
square look sets `--radius` to 0 and squares every step with it, `xl` included.

<Radii />

## Elevation

Tailwind's shadows, by what floats: `xs` under fields and outline buttons, `sm` under cards, `md` under menus and
popovers, `lg` under dialogs and toasts. Borders do most of the separating; a shadow says something is above the page.
