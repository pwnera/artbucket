# Accessibility

What every component holds to, and what the tests check. Each story is run through axe in headless Chromium
(`pnpm test:stories`), and a violation fails it: the fix goes in the component (a label, a role, a token with enough
contrast), never in the test.

## Focus

One ring for everything you can tab to: 2px solid `--ring`, 2px off the element, shown only for the keyboard
(`:focus-visible` in `globals.css`). Fields draw their own, a 3px ring inside their border. Tab through these:

<Frame className="not-prose">
  <TooltipProvider delayDuration={400}>
    <div className="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 rounded-lg border p-4">
      <Button>Upload</Button>
      <Button variant="outline">Cancel</Button>
      <Input aria-label="Search the library" placeholder="Search" className="w-40" />
      <Checkbox aria-label="Required at upload" />
      <Switch aria-label="Approved" />
      <a href="#focus" className="text-sm underline underline-offset-4">A link</a>
      <IconButton label="Edit" variant="ghost">
        <IconPencil />
      </IconButton>
    </div>
  </TooltipProvider>
</Frame>

## Names

- Every control has a name a screen reader can say: its words, its `<Label>`, or `aria-label` on an icon-only one.
  `IconButton` takes `label`, which is both its tooltip and its name.
- Stories query by role and name (`getByRole("button", { name: "Upload" })`), as a screen reader would: a control
  the test can't find that way is a bug in the component.
- Images carry `alt`; a decorative one, `alt=""`.

## Keys

Every frequent action has a key, shown in its tooltip and its menu. `mod` is Cmd on a Mac, Ctrl elsewhere.

<Frame className="not-prose">
  <TooltipProvider delayDuration={400}>
    <div className="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 rounded-lg border p-4">
      <Kbd keys={["mod", "K"]} />
      <Kbd keys={["mod", "B"]} />
      <Kbd keys={["mod", "Z"]} />
      <Kbd keys={["?"]} />
      <Kbd keys={["Esc"]} />
      <IconButton label="Search" shortcut={["mod", "K"]}>
        <IconSearch />
      </IconButton>
    </div>
  </TooltipProvider>
</Frame>

Menus, dialogs, comboboxes and tabs are Radix: arrows move, Enter picks, Escape closes and focus goes back to what
opened them.

## Contrast

Text tokens clear 4.5:1 on every surface text sits on, in both modes (`lib/color.test.ts`, and the table on
[Color](?path=/docs/foundations-color--docs)); the focus ring and an organization's accent clear 3:1. Color never
carries meaning alone: a status says its word, an error its message.

## Motion

Asked for less motion, it all but stops ([Motion](?path=/docs/foundations-motion--docs)). Nothing flashes more than
once a second.

## Screen sizes

Every screen works at 390px wide (the toolbar's Phone viewport): nothing scrolls sideways, and hit targets are at
least 24px, 36px for the main actions.
