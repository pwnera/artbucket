# Color

White and faintly violet greys with a green-black ink in light, neutral greys in dark: tinted neutrals turn muddy on
near-black. Violet (`#6d4aff`) is the primary and marks where you are: the sidebar's current item, a toggle that is
on. Lime highlights selection and what's new, never actions. `accent` stays grey, since it also paints skeletons and
menus.

Every color is a token in `src/app/globals.css` and reaches Tailwind as a color (`bg-primary`, `text-muted-foreground`,
`border-input`). Components are restyled through the tokens, never per component. What follows is read from the CSS
as the app loads it, so it is always the current set.

## Tokens

Each token in both modes, with its value as written and its contrast against what it is read on: a `-foreground`
token on its own surface, anything else on the page's background. Surfaces and borders are not text, so a low ratio
there is expected; text and icons must clear 4.5:1 (3:1 for an icon or a large heading).

## Text on surfaces

Each text token on each surface text sits on. `lib/color.test.ts` holds every one at 4.5:1 or more: a new text
token, or a new surface, joins that test.

## An organization's accent

An organization can set its own accent (Settings, Branding), or follow a brand's (Follow a brand). It replaces
`--primary`, `--ring` and the sidebar's primary, lifted toward white or black until it clears 3:1 on each mode's
background, and `--primary-ink`, lifted to 4.5:1 since it is text (`accentCss` in `lib/color.ts`). The toolbar's
Accent shows the Artbucket brand's own, as the app follows it. Use `primary-ink`, not `primary`, for violet text or a
thin icon.

## Rules

- Restyle through the tokens: no hex or Tailwind palette colors (`text-red-600`) in a component.
- `success`, `warning` and `destructive` say what happened in words too: color is never the only cue.
- A brand's own colors (a site, a swatch) are the brand's: they are drawn as given, not lifted.
