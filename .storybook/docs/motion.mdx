# Motion

One motion language: things arrive on a quick ease-out and leave faster on an ease-in. Motion is decoration, so
asked for less (`prefers-reduced-motion`) it all but stops. What moves and how is CSS (`globals.css`); the helpers in
`src/lib/motion.ts` start it from code.

## Easing

- <Token name="--ease-out" />: arriving, and every transition without its own ease (`--tw-ease`).
- <Token name="--ease-in" />: leaving (a row folding away).
- <Token name="--ease-in-out" />: what moves on screen and stays.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.Easing} />

## Durations

| Duration | Where |
| --- | --- |
| <Token name="--default-transition-duration" /> | Every `transition-*` utility: hovers, colors, a row collapsing |
| 200ms | View transitions, a `details` folding open, a checkbox's check drawing in, a dialog fading out |
| 250ms | A shake |
| 600 to 800ms | A chart drawing in, a breakdown's bars growing, a number counting up |
| 1.6s | A flash fading |

## Helpers

### flash()

Where something just landed, changed or came back lights up and fades: a replaced asset, a rule you jumped to, the
rows a refresh brought in (`useFlashNew`). A tile with its own ground rings instead.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.Flash} />

### shake()

A small no: what was typed was refused, an empty name or a wrong password.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.Shake} />

### collapse() and undo

A row on its way out folds its height away, so the rows below close the gap instead of jumping into it; a review
decision leaves in its color. A soft delete offers Undo for 8 seconds, and Cmd/Ctrl+Z while the toast shows
(`undoable` in `lib/undo.ts`).

<Canvas of={MotionStories.CollapseAndUndo} />

### transition()

Runs a change as a view transition: what it moves glides from where it was (elements keep their identity by
`data-vt`), the rest crossfades. Never under a modal, and plain with less motion. `Morph` moves one thing between two
places, a tile and the viewer.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.Rearrange} />

### useCountUp()

A score or a total counts to its value, from where it was after the first time.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.CountUp} />

## Less motion

With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, animations and transitions run in 1ms, view transitions and press scaling are
off, and `still()` tells code to skip what it would animate. A spinner keeps turning, since it is a status, not
decoration, and delays stay, since a delay is not motion.
