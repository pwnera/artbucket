# Brief: Godot Engine

Build the brand site for Godot Engine, the free and open source game engine, an unofficial showcase. Godot and its robot logo are trademarks of the Godot Foundation; the logo is CC BY 4.0 by Andrea Calabró.

- Tagline: "Free, open source and community-made. Everything you need to put the robot on your page, the right way."
- Voice: friendly, direct, a little playful; made by the people who use it. First person plural. Never corporate, never "enterprise". Avoid "revolutionary", "next-gen", exclamation marks in body text.
- Colors: Godot Blue #478cbf (the robot's head; the brand's color: logo, links, headers, anything that says Godot at a glance), Charcoal #414042 (the wordmark's dark grey, text on light grounds), Sky #6bb3e6 (a lighter blue for tints and hover), Deep Blue #355d83 (a band color, with white text), Paper #eaeef2 (a light ground), Editor Dark #202531 (the editor's ground; dark pages), White #ffffff.
- Type: Montserrat for headings (700), Open Sans for text (all on Google Fonts).
- Logo: the robot head with the wordmark, at https://godotengine.org/assets/press/logo_vertical_color_light.svg (light grounds) and https://godotengine.org/assets/press/logo_vertical_color_dark.svg (dark grounds); the icon alone at https://godotengine.org/assets/press/icon_color.svg. Clear space: the height of the robot's eye on every side. Never redraw the robot, stretch it, or put it on a busy picture.
- Sections the site needs: focus on making your game (welcome), voice, color, typography, logo with do and don't, the robot in product (editor screenshots you can describe, not fetch), community and resources (godotengine.org, godotengine.org/press, foundation.godotengine.org).
