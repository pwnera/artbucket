# Brief: Blender

Build the brand site for Blender, the free and open source 3D creation suite, an unofficial showcase. Blender and the Blender logo are trademarks of the Blender Foundation; the logo may be used to refer to Blender, with its original colors and proportions. Open movie stills are CC BY, credited to the Blender Foundation.

- Tagline: "The Freedom to Create."
- Boilerplate: "Blender is the free and open source 3D creation suite. Free to use for any purpose, forever."
- Voice: plain, confident and generous. Blender speaks like an artist who is glad to share: free to use for any purpose, forever, made by a community for everyone. Say "free and open source", both words, every time. Avoid hype and exclamation marks.
- Colors: Blender Orange #e87d0d (the logo's ring; the accent and buttons), Blender Blue #265787 (the logo's core; headers, links), Interface Blue #2d8ceb (hover and highlights), Studio Night #232323 (dark grounds, the interface), Paper #f5f5f5, White #ffffff.
- Type: Inter for everything (headings 700, text 400), on Google Fonts; Dejavu Sans Mono for code.
- Logo: the mark at https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0c/Blender_logo_no_text.svg and the wordmark at https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3c/Logo_Blender.svg. Clear space of the mark's inner circle on every side; minimum 32px; never recolor, never tilt, never add a drop shadow, never put it on a photo without a scrim.
- Sections the site needs: welcome (with a still from an open movie, which you may describe rather than fetch), voice and boilerplate, color, typography, logo with do and don't, credits (how to credit Blender in a film's titles: "Made with Blender"), resources (blender.org, blender.org/about/logo, studio.blender.org).
